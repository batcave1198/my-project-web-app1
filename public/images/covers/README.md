# Cover Photos for Ceylon Herbs and Spices

Upload your photos here and rename them to match the filenames below. 
After uploading, just tell me which files you added/renamed and I'll wire them to the correct pages.

## Suggested file names

| File Name | Used On | Suggested Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home-hero.jpg` or `home-hero.webp` | Home page - Hero section | `1920 × 1080` (16:9) or `1600 × 900` | Best: Natural spices/herbs on a clean or warm background |
| `shop-hero.jpg` or `shop-hero.webp` | `/shop` page - Top banner | `1600 × 500` | Clean, minimal. Avoid busy text-heavy images |
| `categories-hero.jpg` or `categories-hero.webp` | `/categories` page - Top banner | `1600 × 500` | Looks great with arranged spices/cinnamon quills |
| `about-hero.jpg` or `about-hero.webp` | `/about` page - Top banner | `1600 × 500` | Warm, authentic feel |
| `contact-hero.jpg` or `contact-hero.webp` | `/contact` page - Top banner | `1600 × 500` | Simple, bright & clean |
| `wishlist-cart-hero.jpg` or `wishlist-cart-hero.webp` | `/wishlist`, `/cart` pages (optional) | `1600 × 400` | Subtle, low-detail works best |

## Logo files

Put your custom logo in `public/logo/` with these names:

| File Name | Used On | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.png` | Header (main logo) | **Recommended.** Can be transparent or on solid. PNG or WebP also fine. |
| `logo-white.png` | Dark backgrounds (future use) | Optional |
| `logo-mark.png` | Icon/small mark (future use) | Optional - square mark works best |

**Tips:**
- Use `.webp` if possible - smaller file size, loads faster
- Keep hero images around 200-800KB for best performance
- Don't add text/logos to cover photos - I'll handle branding
- I won't change your overall design, just add image slots where they fit naturally
